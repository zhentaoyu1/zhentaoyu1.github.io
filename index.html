About Me

I am a Senior Researcher at Tencent Hunyuan in Shanghai, currently working on interactive video world models in the Video World Model Team. My research interests include world models, video editing, subject-driven (reference-to-video) generation, joint audio-video generation, and post-training of video generation models.

At Tencent Hunyuan, I worked on Hy-Nomas, an open-source interactive video world model, where I was responsible for diffusion distillation (reducing sampling from 100 to 4 NFE per segment) and a PixelUMM-based pixel-space diffusion decoder. Before that, I led the development of HunyuanCustom, a multimodal-driven customized video generation framework (1.2k+ GitHub stars), and the multi-condition control model of HunyuanVideo, which powers animated stickers in QQ, Yuanbao and Sogou Input Method.

Earlier, I worked on virtual human generation at Xiaobing (XiaoIce) and on human detection and 3D pose estimation at YITU Technology. I received my M.Eng. and B.Eng. degrees from Zhejiang University.
