Video world models & generative AI

Zhentao Yu

Senior Researcher · Tencent Hunyuan

Creating visual worlds.
Making them interactive.

I work on interactive video world models in Tencent Hunyuan’s Video World Model Team. My research spans video editing, subject-driven generation, joint audio-video generation, and post-training.

Previously, I worked on virtual human generation at XiaoIce and human perception at YITU. I received my bachelor’s and master’s degrees from Zhejiang University.
